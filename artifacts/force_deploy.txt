 Use Railway’s venv-managed gunicorn entrypoint instead of `python -m gunicorn`

Why:
Railway’s launcher provisions a virtualenv and shims the `gunicorn` binary there.
Running `python -m gunicorn` bypasses those shims, causing the app to start under the wrong interpreter and fail at runtime.

What:
Procfile → `web: gunicorn 'adapter.factory:create_app()' --bind 0.0.0.0:$PORT`

Result:
Railway executes the shimmed `gunicorn` inside its venv, ensuring packaged deps are available and the factory app imports correctly.
