## 5. Taxonomy and the six shared-Gate cases

### Distinct structures, not interchangeable names

A **circuit group** is a family of circuits. RAVE's ordinary grouping pairs Knowing/Centering, Understanding/Sensing and Ego/Defense. A **circuit** is a particular connected mechanical organization. A **stream** joins particular Channels within a circuit; RAVE's “Streams” discussion explicitly uses Logic and Acceptance together as the Stream of Understanding. A stream therefore is not simply another name for a circuit. [RAVE, PDF25/printed2 and PDF34–35/printed11–12](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1jDumjx09Ybta84GM9xXxkuaLN1NTGj6b/view).

**Substream**, as used by this catalog, is the Product field holding its seven allowed labels. It is not a claim the source books consistently call all seven structures “substreams.” In particular, Integration is a system and not one of the six ordinary circuits in the older structural presentation.

### Static classification versus activation

The catalog encodes the fixed Channel and circuit relationships represented in PF08, PF11 and the RAVE diagrams. A chart's activations, or the way two charts jointly supply endpoints, do not relabel the Channel. This is an explicit derivation from the fixed source topology and the separately specified PF01 activation classifier. A Gate's presence is not evidence that its complete Channel is defined; both endpoints and the relevant ownership pattern matter. No transit acquisition, ephemeris, Personality/Design ingestion policy or substructure calculation is introduced.
