## Literal convention

Each OLD, NEW, BEFORE, AFTER and INSERT literal is the text of the lines between its opening and closing fence lines, joined with LF, with no LF added after the last of those lines. A literal that ends with a line ending therefore shows an empty last line before the closing fence. Every fence is four backticks, and no literal contains a run of four backticks. `REPLACE`: the OLD block occurs exactly once in the baseline and is replaced by NEW; nothing outside OLD is consumed. `INSERT`: BEFORE and AFTER each occur exactly once, they are contiguous, the gap is their boundary, and neither changes. Every operation resolves against the unchanged v1.2.5 baseline.
