## Literal conventions

- Each fenced block is a literal. Its bytes are exactly the lines between the opening fence line and the closing fence line, joined by LF, with no LF after the last line. Fences are four backticks; no payload contains a backtick fence.
- Any LF outside a literal is stated in the redline as a count. The two anchors are contiguous in the original; the gap is the boundary between them. The inserted bytes are the INSERT literal followed by the stated number of LF characters.
- For `REPLACE`, the old block is the stated original line's content without its line ending, and the replacement block is the NEW literal. The line ending after the old block stays.
