## The Rave Charts

Overview  
There are three different types of Rave charts; the Individual Rave, the Composite Rave and the Daily Rave. The Rave is a tool for revealing design. Each of these Rave charts are essential to capture and understand how our lives work.

Individual Rave  
Of foremost importance is the Individual Rave. It is a map to one’s own design and reveals clearly how one connects to the world. On the opposite page is an example of an Individual Rave chart.

Wheels and Body Graph  
As discussed earlier and as you can see, there are two wheels, one on either side of a Body Graph. The wheel to the left of the Body Graph is the Wheel of the Design Crystal. The wheel on the right of the Body Graph is the Wheel of the Personality. The Body Graph is the integrative field for the information derived from these two wheels.

How to Obtain a Rave  
Anyone capable of calculating an ordinary astrological chart can easily plot a Rave. For those who are unable to do so, any astrological computer service can provide you with quick and accurate calculations inexpensively. These services exist in all major centers. Two Calculations  
The Rave is based on two sets of calculations, one for each of the wheels. The wheel on the right, the Personality Wheel, is calculated in the same manner as a normal astrological chart. It is necessary to know the exact time, date and place of birth.
