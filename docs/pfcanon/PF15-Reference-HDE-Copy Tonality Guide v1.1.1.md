# **Glow Tonality Guide v1.1: Writing for Human Connection**

**Status:** Reference

**Version:** v1.1.1

**Effective date:** 2025-11-04

**Last Update Gate:** 2025-11-03 narrative pack drain

 **Scope:** How we write about how two people feel together. Public copy is band-only and felt-only. No instructions. No jargon. No em dashes.  
 **Routing (titles-only):** Transport and acceptance live in **HDE-Governance**. Public bytes and examples live in **HDE-CLI-API-Vendor-Ref**. Schema and length rules live in **HDE-Schemas & Artifacts**. Mechanics and tests live in **HDE-Mechanics Guide**.  
 **Out of scope:** Numbers in story text, private diagnostics, UI copy, process policy.

---

## **North star**

**Stories, not prompts.** We describe how it tends to feel together. We do not tell anyone what to do.  
 **Felt-only.** Name climate and pace. Avoid actions, rooms, and body words.  
 **Warm, plain, honest.** Present tense. Short sentences. One idea per sentence.  
 **Safe by design.** No fate talk. No labels. No medical or diagnostic tone.  
 **Symmetric on the surface.** Public text reads the same regardless of order.

---

## **What we write**

**Three views per pair**

* **A with B:** how A often feels around B.

* **B with A:** how B often feels around A.

* **Together:** the shared feel when they are together.

Personal views can hold difference. Shared stays calm and even.

**Units**

* One band narrative per category. Start with **Harmony**. The rest follow the same rules.

* Category micro-voices give each category a distinct feel. Vary phrases to avoid repetition.

---

## **Style rules (must)**

* Present tense. Short, natural sentences. Middle-school reading level.

* Felt words only: *pace, timing, rhythm, ease, focus, warmth, clarity, steadiness*.

* Forbidden: jargon, **tempo** (prefer pace or timing or rhythm), destiny language, blame, instructions, em dashes.

* Parity: public text must read the same for AB and BA.

* Variation: rotate phrasings so two matches do not feel copy-pasted.

---

## **Safety and suppression**

* When timing is not met or pressure is high, say less.

* If signals clash, choose the gentler variant.

* Bands never change due to a flag. Copy softens or stays quiet.

## **Category micro-voices (Magic-10)**

*(Internal mapping only. Never surface mechanics.)*

**Harmony** — calm, steady, kind pace.  
 Seeds: “The pace feels kind and even.” “What matters stays clear without heaviness.”

**Heat** — bright, close, vivid without pressure.  
 Seeds: “Warm and close without push.” “Attention meets at the same point.”

**Communication** — words land; quiet helps.  
 Seeds: “Words land and quiet carries meaning.”

**Alignment** — fair, grounded, facing the same way.  
 Seeds: “It feels like they want the same thing.”

**Comfort** — safe, easy, unforced.  
 Seeds: “It feels safe and welcome.”

**Consistency** — even rhythm, reliable feel.  
 Seeds: “Their rhythm holds, day after day.”

**Expansion** — open, curious, room to grow.  
 Seeds: “There is room to grow without strain.”

**Creativity** — playful, joined ideas that feel alive.  
 Seeds: “Ideas join and feel alive.”

**Drive** — focused momentum that stays human.  
 Seeds: “Momentum builds in a way they can trust.”

**Balance** — give and receive feel even.  
 Seeds: “Give and receive feel fair.”

## **Language patterns**

**Do**

* “It feels calm and steady.”

* “Readiness arrives without pressure.”

* “They part feeling spacious and steady.”

* “What matters stays clear without heaviness.”

**Avoid**

* Advice words: *should, must, try, do this*.

* Moral labels. Replace with neutral states: *when tight, when spiky*.

* Abstract stacks. Keep one idea per line.

* “Tempo.” Prefer *pace*, *timing*, or *rhythm*.

---

## **Rotation banks**

**Harmony closing rotation**

* They leave with more ease than they arrived with.

* They part feeling spacious and steady.

* They end with a calm, open feeling.

**Neutral openers**

* Together they feel clear and unhurried.

* Together they feel easy and steady.

* Together they feel calm and focused.

**Soft middles**

* What matters stays in view without heaviness.

* The pull is warm without pressure.

* The pace settles and holds.

## **Templates**

**A with B (personal)**  
 “**With \[Name\], \[felt change\]. \[What tends to come forward\]. \[State that often remains\].**”

**B with A (personal)**  
 “**With \[Name\], \[felt change\]. \[Noise that drops or focus that appears\]. \[State that often remains\].**”

**Together (shared)**  
 “**Together they feel \[quality\]. \[How clarity or warmth shows up\]. \[Closing from rotation bank\].**”

## **Approved examples**

**Harmony — Nathan and Vanda (shared)**  
 Nathan and Vanda together feel like clarity held at a kind pace. Nathan feels steadier and more honest around Vanda; Vanda feels clearer and less rushed around Nathan. His focus lifts what matters while her timing keeps it human. They meet each other’s timing until what is different settles into a shared rhythm. The lasting sense is simple, grounded, and easy to trust.

**Harmony — Nathan and Tanya (shared)**  
 Nathan and Tanya together feel clear and unhurried. Nathan’s focus feels softer and more honest with Tanya; Tanya’s sense of direction feels recognized and steady with Nathan. Their rhythm is easy, so what matters stays in view without heaviness. The feeling that remains is warm, grounded, and easy to trust.

**Heat — Nathan and Vanda (shared)**  
 Nathan and Vanda together feel bright and close. The pull between them is warm and clear, and attention meets at the same point. Nathan’s clarity carries a glow with Vanda; Vanda’s presence feels bolder with Nathan. It is vivid without pressure and steady enough to trust.

*(When we render personal views, use the same felt-only rules and keep the shared view even.)*

## **Numbers and disclosure**

* Public story is band only.

* If a Details view is tested, show position within band or a small percent with a rules label. Never use numbers in the story text. Keep parity and auditability intact.

---

## **Process alignment**

* Composite-first under the hood. Public copy stays symmetric.

* Directional nuance lives in personal views, not in the shared paragraph.

* Rotate phrasing to avoid repetition. Keep endings from the approved bank.

* Keep timing language visible where it helps. Waiting is care.
