# **1\) Purpose & scope**

PF30.1 is the first rolling PF30 volume. It is the compact, persistent record for governed Change Request Documents (CRDs) from registration through closure.

A CRD is a governed change container comparable to an epic. It begins with an observed need for a feature, defect correction, operational change, infrastructure change, governance change, or another required system change originating outside the Epic/PF09 lane. The Lead Developer analyzes the need and transforms it into a CRD Specification.

One CRD MAY contain one or more PRs, one or more Ops tasks, or both. PF30.1 is designed to contain many concise CRD records and remains active until the Product Owner determines that it is approaching its practical processing limit.

PF30 records CRD identity, bounded scope, phase-separated work, current posture, exact-source references, material decisions, and final disposition. It does not replace the process, plan, implementation, QA, evidence, Ops, Build Notes, or canon artifacts that establish those facts.

# **2\) Authority, ownership & boundaries**

PF30 owns:

* persistent CRD identity and global CRD ID allocation rules;  
* concise registration of the observed need, Lead Developer finding, approved scope, exclusions, dependencies, risks, affected surfaces, and expected outcome;  
* the current recorded CRD status and phase-block structure;  
* references to the approved CRD Specification and the approved change-wide Implementation Plan, planned and completed work units, material ADRs, HDE Build Notes, and exact-source evidence;  
* actual outcome, approved deviations, superseded or rejected scope, residual work, acceptance, and closure disposition;  
* deterministic record order and maintenance within the active PF30 volume; and  
* PF30 volume-status and rollover rules.

PF30 does not govern:

* change-process rules, which belong to **PF06-Canon-Change-Process-Guide**;  
* role permissions, approval authority, and governance rules, which belong to **PF04-Canon-HDE-Governance**;  
* reusable plan and runbook structures, which belong to **PF27-Canon-Plan-Templates**;  
* alchemical phase meanings, which belong to **7 Phases of Alchemical Engineering**;  
* QA procedures and verdict semantics, which belong to **PF19-Canon-Glow-QA-Guide**;  
* schema, artifact, manifest, and evidence contracts, which belong to **PF12-Canon-HDE-Schemas-and-Artifacts**; or  
* Build Notes and canon-drain procedures, which belong to **PF10-HDE-Build-Notes** and the permanent canon documents that own the affected subjects.

The Epic/PF09 lane and the CRD/PF30 lane remain distinct. Epic-originated work remains accountable to the applicable phased HDE Build Checklist volume. CRD-originated work remains accountable to PF30 and MUST NOT be duplicated in PF09 unless the Product Owner explicitly authorizes that mapping.

Within its distinct lane, CRD work follows the full downstream lifecycle governed by **PF06-Canon-Change-Process-Guide**. Implementation MUST NOT begin before CRD Specification approval, and that approval does not by itself authorize implementation; **PF04-Canon-HDE-Governance** governs implementation authority. CRD work MUST receive full independent QA through **PF19-Canon-Glow-QA-Guide**; CI or a lightweight development check does not replace that QA.

A canon-changing CRD MUST have an approved accompanying ADR. PF30 records the ADR, affected canon, and intended drainage targets. Material CRD activity, decisions, deviations, and results are recorded in **PF10-HDE-Build-Notes** and drained to permanent canon through the owning process.

# **3\) CRD record model**

## **3.1 CRD identifier governance**

The canonical top-level CRD ID syntax is `HDE-CRD-<NNNN>`, where `<NNNN>` is a decimal sequence with at least four digits. The sequence begins at `0001`; after `9999`, the numeric component expands without changing the prefix or meaning. The exact syntax is `^HDE-CRD-[0-9]{4,}$`.

The Product Owner is the CRD ID allocation authority. Before assigning an ID, the allocator MUST inspect all PF30.x volumes and assign the next integer after the highest CRD ID already entered. If no CRD ID has been entered, the first numeric component is `0001`.

Each CRD ID:

* MUST be globally unique across all PF30.x volumes;  
* MUST NOT be reused after entry, including after rejection, cancellation, supersession, or closure;  
* MUST remain immutable after entry; and  
* MUST remain independent of the PF30.x volume containing the record.

The hyphenated `HDE-CRD-` syntax identifies the top-level PF30 CRD model. It does not adopt or revive the superseded epic-internal `HDE-CRD0XX` meaning.

## **3.2 Record lifecycle**

Each CRD has one primary PF30 record. Records are ordered by the numeric CRD ID sequence. A later update MUST modify the same CRD entry in the same PF30.x volume unless an authorized rollover requires a reciprocal continuation record.

“Update in place” means updating the same CRD entry within its containing PF30.x volume. Publishing the revised PF30.x artifact remains subject to normal PF document versioning. A material update does not create a second primary CRD record.

Every CRD record MUST identify its originating PF30.x volume. When an authorized split occurs, the record MUST also identify its continuation PF30.x volume and reciprocal continuation reference. Before a split, the continuation fields use `Not applicable`.

A rollover alone does not move a CRD record. An unsplit CRD continues to receive all required updates in its originating volume. Updates continue in a later volume only after an explicit reciprocal continuation is created; that continuation does not create a second primary CRD record.

An intake enters PF30 only after the Lead Developer finds that the need is valid, is not already satisfied, and properly belongs in the CRD lane. If any finding is negative, the intake MUST NOT receive a CRD ID or PF30 record. Its disposition remains in the authoritative intake or decision source governed by **PF06-Canon-Change-Process-Guide**.

After all three findings are positive, the Product Owner allocates the CRD ID and the initial PF30 record is entered. CRD Specification approval remains a later, separately recorded decision. Implementation MUST NOT begin while that approval is pending or rejected.

A registered CRD that is rejected, withdrawn, cancelled, or superseded before any implementation candidate is created and before any Ops task is executed MUST be closed through the administrative terminal path without an executed QA cycle. The record MUST use these exact values:

* **Implementation references:** `Not applicable — no implementation candidate created.`  
* **Ops references:** `Not applicable — no Ops task executed.`  
* **QA requirement:** `Not applicable — terminated before implementation.`  
* **Independent QA Plan:** `Not applicable — terminated before implementation.`  
* **QA execution:** `Not applicable — no candidate existed.`  
* **Remediation and QA re-execution:** `Not applicable — no QA cycle began.`  
* **Final QA verdict and exact candidate:** `Not applicable — no candidate existed.`  
* **Acceptance decision:** `Not applicable — administrative termination before implementation.`

The terminal status, closure date, decision owner, decision date, authoritative termination source, actual outcome, and any successor CRD reference remain required. This administrative path MUST NOT be used if an implementation candidate was created or an Ops task was executed. In that case, all applicable QA, remediation, evidence, acceptance, and closeout obligations remain in force even if the CRD is later cancelled or superseded.

The record has three postures:

* **Initial registration:** records the observed need, Lead Developer finding, bounded scope, CRD Specification and approval posture, phase structure, dependencies, risks, affected surfaces, QA requirement, and current blockers.  
* **Material change:** updates affected current-state fields and appends one traceability row when a material scope, status, authority, phase, dependency, work-unit, canon, ADR, QA, blocker, or expected-outcome change would otherwise make the record misleading.  
* **Closure:** records exact-source implementation, Ops, QA, remediation, acceptance, canon, and closure lineage together with the actual outcome and unresolved residue.

PF30 MUST NOT invent or infer implementation, QA PASS, Ops completion, acceptance, canon drainage, or closure. It MUST reference authoritative evidence instead of copying full evidence payloads.

## **3.3 Controlled status vocabularies**

CRD-record status and phase-block status are separate vocabularies. A record MUST use the exact value and capitalization defined below. Synonyms and local variants are prohibited.

### **CRD-record status**

| Value | Meaning |
| :---- | :---- |
| `Registered` | The intake threshold is satisfied, an ID is assigned, and CRD Plan approval is not yet recorded as Approved. |
| `Plan Approved` | The CRD Plan is approved and execution has not begun. |
| `In Progress` | Approved implementation or Ops work is underway. |
| `In QA` | An exact implementation or Ops candidate is undergoing independent QA. |
| `In Remediation` | Approved remediation is underway after a QA or evidence finding. |
| `Blocked` | An unmet dependency or unresolved condition currently prevents progress. |
| `Deferred` | An authorized decision has paused the CRD without terminating it. |
| `Closeout Pending` | All approved implementation and Ops work and required QA are complete, but one or more acceptance, drainage, residual-work, or closure decisions remain. |
| `Closed` | The executed change has received an authorized closure decision and all claim distinctions are recorded. |
| `Rejected` | The registered CRD Plan was rejected and the CRD received an administrative terminal closure. |
| `Withdrawn` | The registered change request was withdrawn. The no-candidate exception applies only when the conditions in §3.2 are satisfied. |
| `Cancelled` | Authorized termination ended the CRD. The no-candidate exception applies only when the conditions in §3.2 are satisfied. |
| `Superseded` | Another authorized change replaced the CRD. The successor reference is recorded, and the no-candidate exception applies only when the conditions in §3.2 are satisfied. |

`Closed`, `Rejected`, `Withdrawn`, `Cancelled`, and `Superseded` are terminal CRD-record statuses. A terminal record MUST NOT return to a non-terminal status. A renewed change need receives a new CRD ID and references the terminal predecessor when applicable.

### **Phase-block status**

| Value | Meaning |
| :---- | :---- |
| `Planned` | The phase block is defined but is not ready to begin. |
| `Ready` | Its dependencies are satisfied and its work may begin when authorized. |
| `In Progress` | At least one work unit in the phase block is underway. |
| `Blocked` | An unmet dependency or unresolved condition prevents the block from advancing. |
| `Deferred` | An authorized decision has paused the block without terminating it. |
| `Completed` | The explicit phase-block completion boundary is satisfied and exact-source completion references are recorded. |
| `Cancelled` | Authorized termination ended the phase block. |
| `Superseded` | An authorized replacement made the phase block no longer operative. |

Phase-block `Completed` does not establish CRD `Closed`. A change to either controlled status is material and MUST be reflected in the current-state field and append-only material-change history.

# **4\) Minimum CRD record contract**

## **4.1 Initial registration**

An initial CRD record MUST contain:

| Field | Minimum recorded content |
| :---- | :---- |
| CRD identity | Stable CRD ID, title, controlled CRD-record status, and creation date |
| CRD continuity | Predecessor CRD and successor CRD IDs with exact record references, or `Not applicable` |
| Volume continuation | Originating PF30.x volume, continuation PF30.x volume, and reciprocal continuation reference |
| Observed need | Concise observed condition or requested change |
| Business case | For each requirement: its beneficiary, who gains (Glow users, the production operator, or the delivery team); its evidence, the cited observed incidents or measurements that show the problem (a forecast is not evidence); its benefit, what changes for the beneficiary in user outcome, risk, time, or money; and its measure, how the benefit is checked after delivery |
| Affected surfaces | Applicable code, data, operations, documentation, governance, and canon surfaces |
| Constraints and risks | Known constraints, risks, and safety or compatibility boundaries |
| Intake evidence | Available exact-source intake evidence or `None available` |
| Lead Developer finding | Explicit finding that the need is valid, is not already satisfied, and properly belongs in the CRD lane |
| Lead Developer disposition | Bounded scope, exclusions, and expected outcome; approval is established only by the Specification approval fields |
| Specification approval | CRD Specification reference, approval status, decision date, decision source, and approver |
| Phase structure | One or more alchemical phase blocks with purpose, bounded scope, dependencies, completion boundary, and controlled phase-block status |
| Work units | `Pending — identified by the change-wide Implementation Plan`. The Specification does not prescribe PR or Ops units; once the change-wide Implementation Plan is approved, a material update references it and records its PR and Ops units with their phase allocation |
| Dependencies and blockers | Dependency order, current blockers, and compatibility dependencies |
| Canon posture | Canon impact; affected canon and intended drainage targets; accompanying ADR reference and status when applicable |
| QA posture | Full independent QA requirement and independent QA Plan reference or current planning status |
| Build Notes | Reference to each material HDE Build Notes decision, or `None recorded` |

A Specification approval record does not establish implementation, QA, Ops, acceptance, canon drainage, or closure.

## **4.2 Material update**

A material change MUST be recorded when it affects:

* CRD-record or phase-block status;  
* predecessor or successor CRD reference;  
* volume continuation;  
* approved scope or exclusions;  
* phase structure or completion boundaries;  
* dependency order;  
* planned PR or Ops work units;  
* canon impact or intended drainage targets;  
* ADR posture;  
* QA posture;  
* blocker status; or  
* expected outcome.

The affected current-state fields MUST be updated in the existing CRD entry. The material-change history MUST remain append-only and contain one row per material change:

| Date | Affected fields | Approved change or deviation | Decision source | Build Notes reference |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
|  |  |  |  |  |

Earlier material-change rows MUST NOT be overwritten or deleted. Routine activity, commands, logs, intermediate execution detail, and non-material progress remain in their owning sources; PF30 MUST NOT become a running activity log.

## **4.3 Phase-block tracking**

Each phase block MUST record or reference:

* its alchemical phase;  
* its purpose and bounded scope;  
* its PR and Ops work units, as identified by the approved change-wide Implementation Plan;  
* its dependencies;  
* an explicit completion boundary;  
* its controlled phase-block status; and  
* its actual PR, commit, merge, or Ops references when completed.

Each PR or Ops task MUST belong to exactly one alchemical phase. A CRD MAY contain multiple ordered phase blocks and is not required to traverse all seven phases. Completion of one phase block does not complete the CRD.

## **4.4 Closure update**

A CRD closure update MUST contain the following fields. For an administrative terminal closure with no implementation or Ops execution, the exact no-candidate values in §3.2 satisfy the applicable implementation, Ops, QA, remediation, candidate, and acceptance fields.

* terminal CRD-record status and closure date;  
* implemented PR, commit, merge, and Ops references as applicable;  
* independent QA Plan reference or the exact no-candidate value;  
* QA execution evidence reference or the exact no-candidate value;  
* remediation and QA re-execution references when applicable, or the exact no-candidate value;  
* final QA verdict and exact candidate identity, or the exact no-candidate value;  
* actual outcome;  
* approved deviations;  
* superseded or rejected scope;  
* deferred or residual work;  
* ADR status;  
* canon-drain status and intended targets still outstanding, if any;  
* acceptance decision with decision owner, date, and authoritative source, or the exact no-candidate value; and  
* final closure decision, decision owner, date, and authoritative closure source.

Acceptance MUST remain an explicit decision. It MUST NOT be inferred from QA PASS, implementation, merge, Ops completion, canon drainage, or closure.

No implemented change or executed Ops change MAY use the no-candidate administrative values.

At closure, the **CRD status** field in Record control MUST contain the terminal CRD-record status. The date and source in **Closure decision** are the authoritative closure date and authoritative closure reference. PF30 MUST NOT maintain duplicate final-status, closure-date, or closure-reference fields.

# **5\) Evidence and claim posture**

Exact-source evidence is the current evidence model. A PF30 record MUST reference the authoritative PR, commit, SHA, workflow run, QA, Ops, artifact, ADR, HDE Build Notes, acceptance, and closure sources applicable to each claim without reproducing their complete payloads.

Mandatory acceptance-token fields, rosters, claims, and token-evidence matrices are not required. Historical token data MAY remain as historical, optional metadata when preserved by its owning source or compatibility schema. A historical token is not current acceptance proof and MUST NOT replace exact-source evidence or a scoped acceptance decision.

PF30 preserves these distinctions:

* A PR reference does not by itself prove implementation at a later repository state, QA acceptance, Ops completion, canon drainage, acceptance, or closure.  
* A commit or SHA identifies repository state; it does not by itself prove CI, QA, artifact integrity, acceptance, or closure.  
* A QA verdict does not by itself prove Ops completion, canon drainage, acceptance, or closure.  
* An Ops completion reference does not by itself prove repository implementation, QA acceptance, canon drainage, or CRD closure.  
* A Build Notes entry does not by itself prove repository implementation or permanent canon drainage.  
* An ADR authorizes its bounded canon decision; it does not by itself prove implementation, drainage, acceptance, or closure.  
* An acceptance decision does not erase unresolved Ops, drainage, residual-work, or closure state.  
* A closure decision MUST preserve the recorded state of implementation, QA, Ops, canon drainage, acceptance, and residual work as separate claims.

The underlying evidence, QA, acceptance, and closure contracts remain owned by **PF04-Canon-HDE-Governance**, **PF06-Canon-Change-Process-Guide**, **PF19-Canon-Glow-QA-Guide**, and **PF12-Canon-HDE-Schemas-and-Artifacts**.

# **6\) Rolling-volume maintenance**

The allowed PF30 volume-status values are:

* **Pending activation:** the volume is under review and MUST NOT accept CRD registrations.  
* **Active:** the sole volume accepting new CRD registrations.  
* **Closed to new CRDs:** no new CRD may be registered, but every contained record remains authoritative and may receive all required material, correction, continuation, and closure updates.

PF30.1 is the first PF30 volume and is Active at initial canonical publication. Before authorized canonical publication, a review draft MUST use **Document status** `Draft` and **Volume status** `Pending activation`. Initial canonical publication MUST change those fields to `Canon` and `Active` in the same authorized version.

Only one PF30.x volume MUST be Active for new CRDs at a time. Only a PF30.x volume whose document status is Canon and whose volume status is Active may accept a new CRD registration.

The active volume holds many CRD records. No fixed CRD count triggers rollover, and a CRD does not receive its own PF30 document. Closed records MUST be retained.

Rollover occurs only when the Product Owner determines that the active volume is approaching its practical digital-processing limit. The next volume after PF30.1 is PF30.2. Later volumes continue sequentially as PF30.x. The document-version suffix for a future volume is established when that volume is created.

A CRD SHOULD remain in one volume where practical. Opening PF30.2 closes PF30.1 only to new registrations. An unsplit active CRD remaining in PF30.1 MUST continue receiving every required material and closure update in PF30.1.

Updates move to a later volume only when the Product Owner authorizes a split and an explicit reciprocal continuation is created. Both volumes MUST identify the unchanged CRD ID, the originating and continuation volumes, and the reciprocal continuation reference. The CRD ID remains independent of volume identity.

When a successor opens, the prior volume's **Volume status** changes to `Closed to new CRDs`. The prior volume's **Next volume** field and the successor's **Previous volume** field MUST identify each other. The prior volume remains an authoritative historical record; the successor does not semantically supersede it. Prior volumes and closed records MUST NOT be removed.

Document status and volume status are separate. Publishing a new version of a PF30.x document does not by itself open, close, activate, or supersede a volume. Initial activation requires the authorized canonical-publication transition defined above.
